# Changelog

## Version 3

- With boost off, the regular volume slider now stops at 100% too. Louder
  switches GNOME's "Over-Amplification" setting off while boost is off, and
  puts it back as it was when Louder is turned off or removed.

## Version 2

- Click the slider's speaker icon to turn the boost on or off.
- With boost off, the slider is greyed out and any volume above 100% drops
  back to 100%.
- Louder remembers whether boost is on after logging out or locking the screen.

## Version 1

- First release: a Quick Settings slider that sets the volume from 0% to 300%,
  with the percentage shown and a snap to 100%.
